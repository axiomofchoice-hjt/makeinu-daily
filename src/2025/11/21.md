## 3. 协程可以把异步回调地狱的逻辑给它展平了

复杂 io 操作要 `callback(callback(callback))`，展平成：

```text
auto ret1 = co_await task1()
auto ret2 = co_await task(ret1)
auto ret3 = co_await task(ret2)
```
